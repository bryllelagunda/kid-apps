🔊 HIT 7
One gesture (P2): finger down anywhere in your slice of the world (not a small hotspot), pull back, the dotted arc updates live, lift to fire. Direction + distance = angle + power in a single touch. Ambient learning (P4): every block wears a letter or number all the time. The glowing block + "HIT 7" pill is the bonus target — always a block on an opponent's castle, so it can never collide with the "OOPS, own castle" rule. Missing it costs nothing. Three hearts, not a health bar, so damage is countable (§28). The ⏸ is the only mid-match affordance: it is the sole entry to PAUSED, to the parent settings panel, and to abandoning a match.

§24 everyone-gets-something: every player gets exactly one award, assigned greedily. 🏆 CHAMPION always goes to the match winner, never to the high scorer — the win condition is last-player-standing, so a score-based champion could print two different names on this one screen. Awards with a zero-valued stat are skipped in favour of 🎈 GOOD GAME. 🖨 POSTER (M5) is Codex's idea — freeze the wreckage with canvas.toDataURL() and offer print or save; window.print() alone is unreliable from an installed iOS PWA.
